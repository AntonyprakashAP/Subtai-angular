Your Step-by-Step Guide to Subtitle Generation

Welcome to our comprehensive tutorial! Follow these simple steps to effortlessly generate accurate subtitles for your videos. No prior experience needed!

Step 1: Upload Your Video File

This is where your subtitle journey begins!
  • Click the "Get Started" button below or drag and drop your video file into the designated area.
  • We support a wide range of popular video formats (e.g., MP4, MOV, AVI).
  • Your video will be securely uploaded to our servers for processing. Please ensure you have a stable internet connection.

Step 2: Choose Source & Target Languages

Accuracy starts with the right language selection.
  • Source Language: Select the primary language spoken in your uploaded video. This helps our AI accurately transcribe the audio.
  • Target Language(s): Choose the language(s) you want your subtitles to be translated into. You can often select multiple target languages for broader reach.
  • Our platform supports over 50 languages, ensuring global accessibility for your content.

Step 3: Review & Download Your Subtitles

Your subtitles are ready!
  • Once the processing is complete, you'll see a preview of your generated subtitles.
  • You'll have options to download them in various formats, such as SRT (SubRip Subtitle) or VTT (WebVTT), which are widely compatible.
  • Click the "Download" button for your preferred format, and your subtitle file will be saved to your device.

Tips for Best Results

  • Clear Audio: Ensure your video has clear and audible dialogue for the most accurate transcription.
  • Single Speaker: For initial attempts, videos with a single speaker can yield better results.
  • Proofread: While our AI is highly accurate, a quick review of the generated subtitles can catch any minor discrepancies.
  • Internet Speed: A faster internet connection will expedite the upload and download processes.
